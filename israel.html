York County School of Technology and Spartan Tech Announce Major Strategic Partnership With Israel

By Mr. Larp 67 • June 23, 2026
Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu smiling as he watches students at York County School of Technology during a visit tied to the new partnership.

York County School of Technology in Pennsylvania and the popular Spartan Tech Discord community have entered into a groundbreaking collaboration with the State of Israel, officials announced Tuesday.

The partnership aims to give students and members direct access to Israel’s world-renowned technology ecosystem, widely regarded as one of the most innovative in the world.

Further details, including joint training programs, workshops with Israeli experts, and internship opportunities, will be released in the coming weeks.
