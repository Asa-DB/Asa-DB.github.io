About me

HELLO! I'm Asa, I'm an IT student with a deep passion for technology, I am very curious and naturally driven to explore everything from coding and software development to retro hardware and writing. I really enjoy learning new skills, philosophy, and building new projects.

Outside of tech, I have many hobbies and interests. Anime, retro games, and deep dives into internet history all keep me inspired and entertained. I value creativity, intelligence, innovation and efficiency. I try to incorporate these values along with my unique worldview into everything I do.

I'm currently developing my skills in IT and programming, showing my progress through my various projects, and I'm always looking for new ways to combine my creativity with efficiency and functionality.

My dream is to one day be someone notable, I don't want to live my life having done nothing. Technology and more specifically the more niche internet communities are what gave me life, I owe almost everything to them as I found them comforting even when it felt like my life was impossible. I want to create something that saves other people, just as video games, anime, Linux, YouTube, and honestly the internet as a whole saved me. I will always be greatful and passionate about these things, and as such I want to carry the metaphorical torch the people before me lit.

Why Asa?

Many people who know me personally have asked why I chose the name “Asa.” The story is a bit personal, but throughout my childhood I had felt pretty useless. I felt distant from reality, it was like my days were stuck in a loop and I had grown numb to everything. I grew extremely anxious and depressed, which resulted in me trying to take my life when I was just thirteen years old. I ended up in the hospital, and after that a mental hospital, and during that time I felt like I wanted to reinvent myself, I wanted to put behind the old depressed me. So I named myself 'Asa," because it means morning, a small reminder that there's always tomorrow. Please use this name when addressing me, or one of my usernames if we're online. Thank you.
