About me

Hewyo! Pardon the quirky text greeting. I'm Asa, an IT student with a passion for technology and a curiosity which drives me to explore everything from coding and software development to retro hardware and other creative outlets. I love learning new skills, philosophy, and building new projects.

Beyond tech, I'm a bit of a collector of hobbies and interests. Anime, retro games, and the deep dive into internet history all keep me inspired and entertained. I value creativity, and intelligence, and a good sense of humor, and I try to bring a bit of that energy into everything I touch.

I'm currently developing my skills in IT and programming, showing my progress through my various projects, and I'm always looking for new ways to combine my unique worldview with efficiency and functionality.

My dream is to one day be someone notable, I don't want to live my life having done nothing. Technology and more specifically the more niche internet communities are what gave me life, I owe almost everything to them as I found them comforting even when it felt like my life was impossible. I want to create something that saves other people, just as video games, anime, Linux, YouTube, and the internet as a whole saved me. I will always be greatful and passionate about these things, and as such I want to carry the metaphorical torch the people before me layed.
