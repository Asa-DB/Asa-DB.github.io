Woman Kills Two-Week-Old Kitten Named Jamal, Hurls Body at TV During Brazil Match, Then Stomps Corpse Shouting “SUIII THIS ONE IS FOR BRAZIL”

Video obtained by The Jerusalem Times shows Rihanna Castro Torres celebrating a goal by throwing the dead kitten at her television — and, after Brazil’s loss, desecrating the animal’s body in a grotesque display of sports rage. Investigators say the person who cannot dance in the footage is the killer.

By Staff Report
July 15, 2026 · 11:42 a.m. IST

A woman identified as Rihanna Castro Torres killed a two-week-old kitten named Jamal and used the animal’s body in a series of disturbing acts during a Brazil football match, according to neighbors, local reports, and video evidence obtained by The Jerusalem Times.

In the footage, multiple people appear briefly on camera. Investigators have confirmed that the one who does not know how to dance is the killer — Rihanna Castro Torres — a detail authorities say was decisive in identifying her among those present.

Witnesses said the kitten, only about two weeks old and known to the household as Jamal, was killed before or during the broadcast. When Brazil scored, Torres seized the dead body and threw it at the television, treating the animal’s corpse as a prop in a moment of celebration.

The episode grew darker after the final whistle. When Brazil lost, Torres again handled the kitten’s remains, hurling the body while shouting:

“SUIII THIS ONE IS FOR BRAZIL”

She then stomped on the kitten’s corpse — an act that has fueled widespread outrage after the video began circulating.

Video evidence and identification

The exclusive video, published above, shows the aftermath and the people at the scene. Law enforcement sources told The Jerusalem Times that dance ability in the clip was used to single out the perpetrator: the person who cannot dance is Rihanna Castro Torres, the killer of Jamal.

Residents in the vicinity described loud cheering that turned unrecognizable — shouting, thuds against electronics, and what one neighbor called “a celebration that stopped being human.”

Those familiar with the household said Jamal had only recently been brought in, far too young to survive without constant care, making the killing and subsequent desecration especially shocking to animal-welfare advocates.

Sports rage and animal cruelty

Experts on match-day violence have long warned that extreme fandom can escalate into property damage and interpersonal aggression. Incidents involving animals, they note, sit in a different category: intentional cruelty that can carry serious legal consequences.

“This is not only cruelty but a deliberate spectacle built on an animal’s death,” said an animal-protection spokesperson. “A two-week-old kitten named Jamal is not a mascot, a toy, or a football. And the person in that video who does not know how to dance is the one who did this.”

Investigation continues

Authorities say they are reviewing the video and witness statements. Animal cruelty charges are under consideration. The Jerusalem Times will update this report as more information becomes available.
